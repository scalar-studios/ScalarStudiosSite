Vinland: 1000

Lead a Norse colony in Vinland across 50 years of survival, politics, relationships, and faith. Make difficult choices to manage food, shelter, defense, popularity, and the colony's shifting allegiance between the old gods, Rome, and Constantinople.

About the Game

A choice-driven management and narrative strategy game about leading a Norse colony in Vinland. You are handed one event at a time, each with a small set of choices, and every decision shapes the colony's future.

Manage food, shelter, defense, popularity, and the fragile relationships that hold your settlement together. Guide your family and influence your heir, whose choices and ambitions may determine the legacy you leave behind.

Navigate the political climate between your colony and the native peoples of Vinland. At the same time, watch your colony's faith slowly change as the old Norse gods contend with Christianity, whether through Rome, Constantinople, or the traditions your people refuse to abandon.

Across 50 in-game years, your choices shape not only whether the colony survives, but what kind of society it becomes... and what you leave behind.
