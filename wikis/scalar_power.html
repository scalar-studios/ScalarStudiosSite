Items

Dusts
Dusts are a crafting material that can be obtained through grinding materials in the Grinder, with a few exceptions.
Redium Dust and Steel Dust have alternative crafting recipes to obtain them without having the material itself, but its components.
Sawdust cannot be obtained with the Grinder, it is instead a byproduct of cutting wood in the Sawmill, with a chance to obtain it when cutting any type of wood.
  • Coal Dust
  • Cobalt Dust
  • Copper Dust
  • Diamond Dust
  • Emerald Dust
  • Ender Alloy Dust
  • Gold Dust
  • Iron Dust
  • Obsidian Dust
  • Redium Dust
  • Steel Dust
  • Sawdust
Chunks
Chunks are a crafting material that can be obtained through macerating ores in the Macerator.
Furthermore, chunks can be processed into dusts in the Grinder.
  • Cobalt Chunk
  • Copper Chunk
  • Gold Chunk
  • Iron Chunk
Ingots
  • Cobalt Ingot
  • Ender Alloy Ingot
  • Redium Ingot
  • Steel Ingot
Resin Pellets ⇀ Resin Clumps (Vanilla) ⇀ Rubber
Resin Pellets can be extracted from any non-pale wood, while vanilla Resin Clumps can only be extracted from pale wood in the Extractor.
Four Resin Pellets can be combined in a crafting table to create a Resin Clump.
Resin Clumps can be processed into Rubber by extracting it from the Resin in the Extractor.
  • Resin Pellet
  • Rubber

Tools

Cobalt Tools
Cobalt Tools are a set of tools, just as powerful as netherite tools, but are powered by SPUs.
  • Cobalt Sword
  • Cobalt Pickaxe
  • Cobalt Axe
  • Cobalt Shovel
  • Cobalt Hoe
  • Cobalt Spear
Wrench
The wrench is a tool that can be used to set the I/O functionality of cables and pipes.
